Privacy Policy

InkPanel is self-hosted. Panel configuration and provider credentials are stored on the InkPanel server you operate rather than in a hosted InkPanel account.

Traffic widget

If you configure the optional Traffic widget, the From and To locations you enter are sent from your InkPanel server to the Google Maps Platform Routes API so Google can calculate the requested route and traffic-aware duration. InkPanel does not write returned Google Routes content to its persistent source cache.

The Google Maps API key is stored as a server-side managed secret and is not returned to the browser after it is saved. Google processes information in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Maps Platform terms.

Other optional data sources

When you configure other widgets, InkPanel sends only the information needed to request those services, such as a location for weather, a bus stop code for bus departures, station codes for rail departures, or calendar requests to the calendar URLs you provide.
